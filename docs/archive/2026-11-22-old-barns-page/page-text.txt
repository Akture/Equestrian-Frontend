Bring Akture to your arena
How a barn comes online
We are spacing the first installs about a month apart so each one teaches us something before the next.
We walk the arena
Dimensions, sight lines, power, and where the internet actually reaches. Covered and outdoor rings are scoped separately.
The cameras go in
A wide camera down the long side and one at each end, mounted once and pointed once. Your staff have nothing to charge, carry, or aim.
Your page goes live
equestrian.akture.video and your barn name. You hand out a one-time code; each client account is tied to your barn from then on.
Barn connectivity is the one thing we cannot guess from a photo. Some arenas will need a dedicated uplink or a local recorder on site, and we would rather find that on the walk-through than after the first show.
